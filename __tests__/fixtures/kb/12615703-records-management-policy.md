## Background

The details of our business is:

> **Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd**
> ABN 37 660 747 366;
> Australian Financial Services Licence Number 543 023

This policy is a subset of Financial Advice Co compliance framework, designed to ensure that Financial Advice Co and its Authorised Representatives meets its obligations under the law and act in a manner consistent with the provision of quality advice to its clients.

## To whom does this policy apply

This policy applies to those Authorised Representatives whom Financial Advice Co have authorised to provide advice under its AFSL.

## Documentation to be retained

ASIC Class Order 14/923 ([https://www.legislation.gov.au/Details/F2016C00928](https://www.legislation.gov.au/Details/F2016C00928)) lays out the key requirements. Financial Advice Co, as an AFSL, has responsibility to ensure that records in relation to the provision of personal advice are retained.

Accordingly, this obligation falls on Financial Advice Co Authorised Representatives. Records of the following matters must be retained:

- Information relied on and actions taken that indicates that the Authorised Representative has acted in the best interest of the client, in relation to the advice provided.
- Where you, as our Authorised Representative know, or reasonably ought to know, that there is a conflict between the interests of the client and the interests of yourself, or an associate of yours or of Financial Advice Co, the information relied on and the action taken to indicate that priority has been given to the client’s interests when giving the advice.

## What documentation must be in a client file?

- All records in relation to client interactions, including (but not limited to):
  - File notes
  - Client correspondence
  - System records
  - Recordings of meetings and telephone calls
  - Application forms
  - Direct debit requests
  - Powers of attorney
  - Withdrawal requests
  - Transfer requests
  - Beneficiary nominations
  - Change of details forms
  - Transaction confirmations
  - Periodic statements
  - Advice documents (e.g. Statement of Advice / Authority to Proceed, Client Fact Find, Client Risk Profile, FDS / Opt In, Records of Advice and Statements of Additional Advice, file notes, emails, voice files, client approved telephone conversations etc.)
  - Copies of AML-CTF Know Your Client identification documents (such as, but not limited to):
    - Driver’s licence
    - Passport
    - Credit card
    - Trust deeds
    - Utility statements
- Electronic communications, including emails, text and social media messaging where relevant to the advice.
- Where credit card, bank details and TFNs are retained they must be masked to prevent their use without client consent.

## Time period for retention

Documentation must be:

- Retained for 7 years after the day the personal advice was last provided to the client (even if provided under another AFSL); and
- accessible by Financial Advice Co at all times during that period in a way that enables us to produce the records to a regulator when required to do so.

Accordingly:

- Financial Advice Co requires all advice documentation, including best interests’ documentation, investment/insurance applications and fee disclosure documents to be saved via group work and CRM.
- Financial Advice Co Authorised Representatives are required to provide these documents to Financial Advice Co when requested by Financial Advice Co, provided the request is within 7 years of the date personal advice was last provided to your client.

> **Note:**
>
> - That this obligation continues even if you are no longer an Authorised Representative of Financial Advice Co.
> - If you leave Financial Advice Co, and are not using our approved systems, you must provide copies of your client files in an agreed format. This obligation includes files of any clients, even if advice was originally provided under another AFSL, where you have since provided advice.

## EDR reliance on record keeping

External Dispute Resolution (EDR) schemes rely on evidence provided by the parties to a dispute. Documents created at the same time as the activity or advice in question are usually given more weight than later recollections of what was said or done.

This means contemporaneous file notes of conversations and actions are critical when a dispute is lodged with them. Authorised Representatives should always confirm verbal instructions from a client in writing, e.g. send them an email after a telephone conversation confirming what was said. Statement of Advice and file notes should always detail how any conflicts between goals, available resources and willingness to take risk are resolved.

## Document retention principles, more broadly

Financial Advice Co and its Authorised Representatives are obliged to:

- Create all records as required by law.
- Retain business records which will reasonably assure the availability of those records when needed for business or regulatory purposes.
- Retain all records and information which the law requires to retain and for as long as the law requires.
- Retain all records and information that may be required in court proceedings or investigations of any type, whether those disputes or investigations are active, pending, threatened, anticipated or merely in contemplation.

Records and information no longer required for business or legal purposes must be destroyed in an appropriate and secure manner.

## Document storage

- All hard copy documents should be kept in files in accordance with a filing/indexing procedure designed to ensure that records can be easily located. The indexing procedure should include:
  - Documents that presently exist
  - Location of files
  - Document owner(s)
  - Documents that have been archived or destroyed
- Documents may be kept in electronic format, although key documents such as trust deeds, statutory declarations and POAs should also be kept in the original hard copy.
- All electronic documents must be stored securely, and backed up and must be maintained for the same time periods as the relevant hard copy documents.
- Storage on portable USB sticks is not allowed given their propensity to get misplaced. Documents from related businesses must be maintained separately.
- Electronic communications such as emails or text messages must also be retained where relevant.

## Disposal/destruction of records

Records and information no longer required for business or legal purposes can be appropriately destroyed.

- Records should only be destroyed after the expiry of their applicable retention period and records should always be disposed of in a confidential and secure way (e.g. via the use of secure document bins, document shredders).
- When disposing of IT equipment, storage devices such as hard drives, flash drives, tapes and the like must be deleted or destroyed in such a way that it makes the data inaccessible and unrecoverable.
- Records of such destruction should be maintained.

As noted above, advice records must be retained for 7 years from the last time personal advice was provided. Note that in practice this may mean permanently, especially where clients are regularly receiving personal advice.
