This section holds Q Wealth's **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)** — step-by-step guides for how we carry out our recurring operational processes.

> **Creating a new procedure?** Start from the **SOP Template (Copy Me)** page below. Use the **•••** menu on that page and select **Copy**, then rename and fill in your procedure. This keeps every SOP consistent.

## Procedures in this section

Published procedures will appear as child pages here. Use the panel above to add a new one.

## Conventions

- **Owner** maintains the accuracy of each procedure; the **Approver** signs off on it.
- Every procedure carries a **version history**, an **effective date** and a **next review date**.
- Review each procedure at least annually, or sooner if the underlying process changes.
