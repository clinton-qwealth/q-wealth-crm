## Legacy Approval Process

Advisers joining Financial Advice Co may have clients invested in platforms and/or financial products that are not available on the Financial Advice Co APL and/or may take on a new client that has an existing investment in a non Financial Advice Co approved product.

Whilst we encourage advisers to review client’s portfolios in alignment with the Financial Advice Co APL, we recognise that this may not always be in the client’s best interests.

Advisers are required to contact Financial Advice Co to obtain guidance on their ability to provide advice and retain products that are not listed on the Financial Advice Co APL.

## Investigating Non-APL Products

In the following circumstances you will need to investigate non-APL products:

- If your client has an existing product that is not on the APL, you must investigate whether that product still meets their needs and objectives. If there is no additional benefit to the client in terms of a price reduction or a specific relevant feature gained by recommending an APL product, you should recommend the client retain their existing product unless its rating no longer meets our minimum research requirements. See our Investment Research Policy.
- If your client requests that you consider a specific product that is not on the APL, you must investigate whether that product meets their needs and objectives. In this case, you should also consider APL products to ensure that the alternative product provides a better outcome for the client than an APL product.
- Where there is a specific feature that is available on a non-approved product that is not available via an APL product, it is important to ensure that the specific feature provided is significant relative to the client's goals and objectives.

In the three cases above specific approval is required unless the product was given approval when the advisers joined Financial Advice Co or one of the exemptions in the Exemptions section below applies.

There is no requirement to investigate products outside the APL to find a cheaper product. Whilst cost is an important consideration, prices change over time and will vary based on the features and functionality required to achieve the client's broader objectives.

## Legacy Product Approval

Advisers joining Financial Advice Co may have clients invested in platforms and/or financial products that are not available on the Financial Advice Co APL and/or may take on a new client that has an existing investment in a non Financial Advice Co approved product.

Whilst we encourage advisers to review client’s portfolios in alignment with the Financial Advice Co APL, we recognise that this may not always be in the client’s best interests.

Advisers are required to contact Financial Advice Co to obtain guidance on their ability to provide advice and retain products that are not listed on the Financial Advice Co APL. Blanket approval may be given at the outset on particular products used by a number of existing clients. Please speak to Financial Advice Co about this process.

Advisers seeking legacy approval for products other than those given a blanket approval on joining Financial Advice Co must provide Financial Advice Co with the following information:

- List of Legacy products.
- External Research Report – Lonsec Rating should be Highly Recommended or Recommended and to stay on list must be Investment Grade. If product doesn’t have a Lonsec rating then Financial Advice Co will consider other external investment research reports such as Zenith, Chant West or Morning Star.
- Product Disclosure Statement.
- The case for approving the product outlining the background and the basis for dispensation.
- When Email completed send Legacy Request Form to [contact@financialadviceco.com.au](mailto:contact@financialadviceco.com.au)

Legacy product approvals do not extend to Direct Equities. Direct Equities advice must be in accordance with the Direct Equity Guidelines.

Legacy Approvals may be granted where a product has met Financial Advice Co requirements.

If the product does not meet Financial Advice Co requirements the legacy request will not be approved and financial advice will be limited to general advice or sell recommendations only.

## How long does an approval last?

Where an approval has been granted, this is valid to include in a client's Statement of Advice within 60 days of the approval e-mail.

An approval for a product not on the approved product list is granted for 15 months from the time the approval is given, provided it was included in an advice document within the initial 60 days.

This means that within the 15-month period, you can provide advice to a client on the approved product any number of times without the need for further approval. However, if you are providing advice on the product or service any time after the 15-month anniversary of the approval, you need to seek further approval for the product or service.

## What happens when a product approval is declined?

In some situations, product approval requests may be declined due to the following reasons:

- Insufficient reasons documented on the product approval form outlining why a comparable product or service on the approved product list is not appropriate for the client.
- There is insufficient information to demonstrate why an approved product does not meet your best interest’s duty for the client.
- If the product or service does not meet the minimum research criteria for approval. Note that the criteria will depend on the type of approval sought.

If an approval is not granted, you will need to tell the client that you are unable to provide advice on that product or service and that an alternative product on the approved product list should be investigated. In the event the client does not want you to investigate an approved product, you should decline to provide advice.

## Exemptions to the standard approval requirement

There are four product categories where the standard approval process does not apply. In these cases, the substitute processes must be followed. You are still required to document your investigation into the product and demonstrate how the recommended product meets your best interests’ duty. This includes considering why an approved product is not appropriate.

### Standalone basic deposit products

Approval is not required for stand-alone basic deposit products as an automatic approval applies. For the product to be classified as a basic deposit product it must meet the Corporations Act definition.

Basic deposit products tend not to be listed on the approved product list. These have been removed on the basis that clients generally have adequate knowledge and awareness about their everyday deposit accounts and the product issuers are APRA regulated. Moreover, they normally pose a lower degree of risk than other financial products.

The exemption from the approval process for stand-alone basic deposit products means that there is no extra documentation required other than your research obligations and the disclosure obligations in your advice document such as a statement of advice.

### Retaining an existing platform

If you are recommending a client retain an existing platform that is not on the approved product list you may not be required to submit a Product Approval. This automatic approval only applies to platforms that are:

- APRA supervised
- Public sector superannuation
- Pension vehicles
- Non super-pension platforms that are issued by a licensed Australian bank or life company or subsidiary thereof.

Planners would only need to seek approval from the Research team for an existing platform that does not meet this definition.

This automatic approval provides you with:

- Approval to retain an existing superannuation (including industry and public sector super funds), pension or investment platform;
- The ability to recommend additional contributions and rollovers;
- The ability to recommend basic index options (excluding enhanced or fundamental index funds) and on the platform; and
- The ability to recommend a move from accumulation to pension within an existing platform.

If any of the underlying investment products held by the client within the platforms are not listed on the approved product list (or are not basic index) then you must apply for approval for each of these underlying products.

Alternatively, you can recommend the client invests in a comparable investment product or platform that is on the approved product list.

If the client holds insurance within the superannuation fund, it is automatically approved. This is only applicable if the insurance is a feature within the fund and does not apply to cases where the insurance is separate, but is paid using benefits within the fund.

### Retaining an existing off platform investment product

In situations where the client has an existing investment product that is on the approved product list but held outside of an approved platform, an automatic approval applies and product approval will not be required.

### Retaining existing insurance policies

If you are recommending the client retain their existing insurance policies then you are exempt from the standard approval process. This general approval allows you to recommend the client retain, increase or make additions to an existing insurance policy that is not on the APL.

The general approval is only applicable to the following insurance types:

- Life
- Total & permanent disablement (TPD)
- Trauma
- Income protection
- Business overhead insurance

## How long does the general approval for automatically approved products last for?

The approval to advise a client to retain an automatically approved product is valid for 15 months from the time you provide your initial advice. You can provide advice to a client on the product any number of times within the 15 months without the need for further approval.

However, if you are providing advice on the product after the 15-months you will need to ensure that it continues to meet the approval criteria outlined above and that there is still a basis to retain the fund.

If the fund no longer meets the automatic approval criteria, the automatic approval no longer applies and the normal approval process must be followed where it is still your recommendation to retain the non-APL superannuation product.
