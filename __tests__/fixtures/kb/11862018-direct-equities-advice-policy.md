## To whom does this policy apply

- This policy applies to those Advisers who are authorised to provide full Financial Advice to retail Clients. Accordingly, this policy does not apply to those advisers who have limited authorisations. Such advisers cannot make investment recommendations of this nature.
- This policy relates to the recommendation of any non-managed direct securities/instruments. These include debt and/or equity securities/instruments, debentures, government stocks and bonds, listed income securities, direct structured investments and hybrid securities.

Note that the normal best interest and disclosure obligations remain when making direct equity recommendations.

## Accreditation guidelines

In recognition that direct investment advice is a specialist area requiring significant education and experience, Financial Advice Co have defined minimum education and experience requirements for Representatives wanting to provide direct investment advice.

- In order to provide advice on direct equities all Authorised Representatives must have successfully met the following requirements:
  - An Approved Specialist Course, or relevant tertiary education and industry specific experience. Must be determined appropriate by Compliance at time of application for accreditation (which may require a Skills Assessment)
- And:
  - DFP Units 1, 3, 4, Advanced DFP Units 7, or
  - Diploma of Financial Services (incl DFP1-8 Pre-2006), or
  - Diploma of Financial Markets, or
  - Relevant tertiary education AND industry specific experience. The qualification must be determined appropriate by Compliance at time of application for accreditation (which may require a Skills Assessment).
- All Authorised Representatives that do not have full authorisation from Financial Advice Co; and/or have not met the education requirements are not authorised to provide advice and recommendations on direct equities, including Exchange Traded Funds.
- Direct equity accreditation qualification must be stated on the Authorised Representative certificate as evidence of accreditation.

## Direct Equity Advice Guidelines

Note that where direct equity advice is provided to a client which results in a transaction, only brokers on Financial Advice Co APL must be used, unless one off approval for an alternate broker is requested and approved.

### Initial Public Offerings (IPO’s) – ≥$250 million

You may provide advice on these offerings where the IPO prospectus:

- Is accompanied by independent research from an independent broker research report recommending the IPO (The broker must be a member of the Australian Stock Exchange); or
- Financial Advice Co house position recommending investment in the IPO.

Note:

- These documents must be provided to the client as part of the advice documentation;
- Lead Manager or Lead Broker reports are not acceptable due to the conflict of interest.

### Hybrids (including IPO’s) – Issuing Company in ASX 50

Where a company in the ASX 50 issues hybrid investments; advisers are able to provide advice and recommendation to clients, provided the following information is included in the SOA, SOA (IBR) or ROA document, as applicable.

- Research report from independent broker, or Financial Advice Co regarding the issue
- Research report on the parent company
- Credit Rating report on the parent company verifying its investment grade credit rating

### Companies in the ASX100

Authorised Representatives accredited for Direct Equities must provide at least one research report for the ASX Listed entity in the SOA. Authorised Representatives can use research reports from the broker being used to transact the trade.

### Companies not listed in ASX100 but are in the ASX200

Authorised Representatives accredited for Direct Equities must provide at least two research reports for the ASX Listed entity in the SOA. Authorised Representatives can use research reports from the broker being used to transact the trade, plus one other research report from another broker or independent source.

### Companies not listed in ASX200 but are in the ASX300 or below

Due to the lack of research coverage for ASX Listed entities below the ASX 200, Authorised Representatives accredited for Direct Equities are not able to provide advice or recommendations regarding these stocks.

### Change in ASX Index

Where equities move from the ASX 200 to the ASX 300, and the Authorised Representative has previously provided advice in respect of the equity (when in the ASX 200) Authorised Representatives are restricted to making either “Hold” or “Sell” recommendations post the date of this change. Such recommendations should be based on at least two brokers’ reports. No new money can be accepted, which includes pre-existing dollar cost averaging and DRP arrangements.

### Nil Advice / Execution Only Trades

Execution only trades can only be used where a client has specifically contacted you and given you a direct instruction to buy or sell a particular direct equity. For further information please refer to the Execution Only Guidelines. Note that it may be preferable to refer them to a broker to arrange the transaction themselves, given the requirement for an execution only trade to be truly on a no advice basis.

### Derivatives

Authorised Representatives are not able to transact or advise on derivatives. Authorised Representatives are able to provide advice on Warrants available by PDS providers outlined on the APL.

### Listed Investment Companies (LIC’s)

Authorised Representatives can provide advice on LIC’s provided they have a market capitalisation ≥$250 million.

## Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs)

Advisers wishing to provide advice in respect of Exchange Traded Funds, must either complete an external approved direct equities accreditation course, or a Financial Advice Co internal accreditation programme, tailored to Exchange Traded Funds. Approved ETFs are listed in the Approved Product List. If a recommendation is in respect of an ETF that is not on the APL, then one off approval must be sought.

Approval is not automatically provided, and will depend on the reasons for the recommendation as well as the underlying ETF.

## Buy-backs, capital raisings, take overs, mergers and other special situations

Where you are accredited to provide advice in respect of direct equities, and your client holds direct equities which are subject to buy-back, capital raising, merger, take over or other special situation then you may only provide advice in respect of those holdings, in the same circumstances as described above e.g.

- if the holding is in the ASX 100, then the advice must be supported by a broker’s report;
- if the holdings in the ASX 200, then the advice must be supported by two broker’s reports, at least one of whom must be independent.

## Authorised representatives not currently accredited

- Authorised Representatives who are not direct equities accredited can refer their direct equity business to an appropriately qualified Authorised Representative in the network or a broker available on the APL.
- Authorised Representatives who are not direct equities accredited cannot carry out Execution Only Trades.
- Authorised Representatives who are not direct equity accredited are not permitted to purchase or trade equities using platforms, even if the platform allows these transactions.
- If a financial product on the APL is listing post capital raising, advisers will need to be accredited for Direct Equities prior to providing advice and recommendations on the product.

## Advice considerations and requirements

- Representatives must have a reasonable basis for all recommendations made. In addressing this, the recommendations made should be considered in light of:
  - The client’s specific needs and objectives
  - Any tax or Centrelink implications
  - The available research
  - The client’s risk profile, investment experience, and existing holdings.

## Strategic long-term investment

- Direct Investment recommendations can only be made for the purposes of addressing long term strategic goals.
- Short-selling strategies or any other active, short-term trading strategies, are not permitted, except with specific one-off approval from Financial Advice Co.

## Documenting the advice

All direct investment advice must be provided in writing. All direct investment advice must contain the following information:

- The basis/purpose of the portfolio (e.g., to provide growth or yield);
- A description of how the recommendation is aligned with the client’s needs, objectives and risk profile;
- The specific risks of investing in direct investments;
- A clear outline of the ongoing service being offered, e.g., how often reviews are to be undertaken of the direct investment portfolio.
- Disclosure of all fees, charges, and remuneration to be incurred or received in relation the recommendation (including brokerage and placement fees payable), should it be implemented.

Note that no individual equity should be over-represented in a client’s portfolio. Overweight holdings need to be discussed with the Financial Advice Co investment team and approval should be obtained.

Normal asset allocation and diversification guidelines should apply.
