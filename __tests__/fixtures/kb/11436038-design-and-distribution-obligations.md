## Design and Distribution Obligations

The design and distribution obligation (DDO) regime is legislation aimed at product providers and distributors of products (such as Authorised Representatives), to ensure that retail clients who acquire their product(s) are those that the product is designed for. Distributors must only distribute products that have a target market determination (TMD) available for the product (unless specifically excluded) and take reasonable steps to ensure distribution is consistent with the TMD.

## What is a target market determination (TMD)?

A TMD is a written document that describes the class of consumers that comprises the target market for a financial product and matters relevant to the product’s distribution and review.

Distributors have certain obligations to ensure that they only recommend products to consumers where that TMD is consistent with the client’s characteristics and risk profile.

Refer to the Appendix for those products included in the DDO framework and excluded products.

### A TMD must be available for all products before any advice or service is provided

- This rule does not apply to excluded products.
- Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd requires that Authorised Representatives only undertake any advising or dealing where a TMD exists. This includes the following advice/services:
  - personal advice
  - general advice
  - execution only services
- A request to use a non-approved product will only be considered if a TMD is available.
- You are responsible for accessing and considering the TMD prior to providing any of the advice/services listed above. TMDs will be available from a number of sources (such as the product provider’s website, financial planning software and research houses) and may vary according to the product.
- The current TMD must be downloaded and retained in your working papers for each product (platform and underlying funds, where relevant) every time the product is recommended and/or a note made in your working papers (covered in the next section). This position will be reviewed in due course.

### TMD must be considered in your advice and services

- You must consider the TMD to ensure your advice and services are in line with the TMD.
- For execution only services, in addition to your existing obligations and responsibilities, if you are aware of the client’s personal situation and believe this to be current and accurate, you may rely on that information to determine if the client’s instruction is suited to the product’s TMD. **If the client circumstances do not meet the TMD then you must not provide the execution only service.** Please note all product execution only transactions that require you to consider a TMD must be lodged via “DDO Reporting” via the Adviser Portal.
- You must consider the TMD as part of the best interest steps in relation to product selection. To demonstrate this, you must make a notation in your working papers that you have considered the TMD, including the version number of the TMD and a link to where this is saved in your files/available; **or** alternatively you must retain a copy of the TMD for each client in the working papers, when a product is utilised (as stated in the previous section). This may occur when making a recommendation for new/additional monies into a product or where conducting an investment switch.

### Outside TMD dealings

- Where a TMD makes reference to the product being suitable in an overall diversified portfolio, the dealing will **not** be considered outside TMD.
- If there is no reference in the TMD to an overall diversified portfolio then if three of the below criteria are met dealing will be considered outside TMD and must be reported:
  - Client’s investment objective is different to the TMD
  - Client’s intended product use is different to the TMD
  - Client’s investment timeframe is different to the TMD
  - Client’s risk and return profile is different to the TMD
- In cases where the product recommendation is not aligned to the TMD your working papers must demonstrate the reason(s) why the product was still appropriate.
- Where dealings occur outside the TMD you must report to Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd as soon as possible and prior to dealing/implementation of the advice.
- Lodge “DDO Reporting” via the Adviser Portal explaining why the product is suitable, even though outside the TMD.
- Product providers may require Authorised Representatives to attest to a number of matters at the time of application/lodgement, such as:
  - the provision of personal advice or non-personal advice
  - the TMD was considered
  - where the dealing is outside the TMD, why it is suitable for the client.
- Outside TMD dealings are not permitted for execution only services.

## Reporting obligations

Certain matters must be reported to product providers at specified times. Outside TMD must be reported to Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd as soon as possible and prior to dealing/implementation of the advice.

Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd will manage reporting to product providers on your behalf and is obliged to report the following:

- Significant dealings relating to outside TMD dealings, must be reported by Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd within **ten business days**. ‘Significant dealings’ will be determined by the licensee and is not defined by the legislation.
- Complaints relating to the product, which will be identified through the complaints resolution process.
- Note that TMDs are also required for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) and that slightly different rules apply to reporting obligations for these products. Requirements are limited to:
  - complying with the obligation to keep records of complaint information; and
  - reporting information that an issuer specifies in the TMD.

Notwithstanding these limitations Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd requires Advisers to report all outside TMD dealing in respect of ETFs to ourselves, so that we can determine whether reporting is required to product issuers.

## Record keeping

Your record keeping obligations specific to DDO includes:

- Retaining copies of TMDs relating to the products utilised as part of your advice/services offered to each client at the time of advice/service, **or**
- Notes in your working papers that indicate the TMD was considered along with the TMD’s version number and link to where the TMD is located.
- Utilising working papers to demonstrate factors considered where the client is outside of the TMD and why the advice is still suitable.
- Retaining records of any dealings outside TMD and any reporting completed to Financial Advice Co Pty Ltd via the Adviser Portal.
